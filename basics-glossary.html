Poker Words, Explained

Every term you'll hear, in plain English.

At the table

Blinds
Forced bets posted before the cards are dealt, so there's always something to play for.
Big blind
The larger of the two forced bets. Also the unit players use to measure everything — "I have 100 big blinds" tells you more than a chip count does.
The button
A marker showing who's the nominal dealer. It moves one seat left each hand and decides the order of play.
Hole cards
Your two face-down cards. Only you see them.
Community cards
The five face-up cards everyone shares.
The flop
The first three community cards, dealt at once.
The turn
The fourth community card.
The river
The fifth and final community card.
Showdown
When remaining players reveal their cards to decide the pot.
The pot
All the chips bet so far in the current hand.
An orbit
One full lap of the button around the table. Everyone has had every seat once.

Actions

Check
Pass without betting. Only possible if nobody has bet yet this round.
Bet
Put chips in first.
Call
Match the current bet.
Raise
Increase the bet, forcing others to match it or fold.
Fold
Give up the hand. You lose whatever you've already put in and nothing more.
All-in
Bet every chip you have.

Hands and situations

A set
Three of a kind made with a pocket pair plus one on the board. Well disguised, which is what makes it valuable.
The nuts
The best possible hand given the cards on the table. It cannot be beaten.
A draw
An incomplete hand that becomes strong if the right card comes. Four to a flush is a flush draw.
Kicker
The side card that breaks a tie when two players have the same pair.
Suited connectors
Two cards in sequence and the same suit, like 8♥ 7♥. Weak on their own, capable of making very big hands.
Pocket pair
Two hole cards of the same rank.
Omaha
A variant where you get four hole cards instead of two and must use exactly two of them. Bigger hands, bigger pots.
Hi-Lo
A split-pot format where the best high hand and the best qualifying low hand share the pot.
Seven Card Stud
An older format with no community cards. Each player gets their own cards, some face up.

Formats and behaviour

Ring game
A table you can join and leave whenever you like. The blinds never increase. On ClubWPT, ring games are played with play chips.
Tournament
Everyone starts with the same chips and plays until one player has them all. Blinds increase on a clock.
Buy-in
The chips you bring to the table when you sit down.
Play chips
The virtual currency for Free Social Poker and Free Social Casino. Everyone gets a starting balance, a daily top-up if you fall below the threshold, and bonus chips over time. Play chips have no cash value.
Position
Where you sit relative to the button, which decides whether you act before or after your opponents.
Tilt
Playing worse because you're frustrated. It's usually quiet — a small drop in patience rather than anything dramatic.
Bluff
Betting with a weak hand to make better hands fold.
Value bet
Betting a strong hand hoping to get called by something worse.
