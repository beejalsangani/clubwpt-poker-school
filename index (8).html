What actually happens at a table

The hands at the top of that list are rare. You could play for a long time without seeing a straight flush. Most pots are won with one pair, two pair, or nothing at all.

So don't wait for a monster. Waiting for premium hands is one of the most common beginner mistakes, and it costs you every pot you fold out of while it isn't happening.

Two rules that settle most confusion

Ties are broken by the highest card. Two players with a pair of nines? The one with the better fifth card wins. That deciding card is called the kicker, and it decides more pots than beginners expect.

Suits don't rank. A flush in spades doesn't beat a flush in hearts. Only the card values matter. If the hands are genuinely identical, the pot is split.
